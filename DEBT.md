# Debt

Quick fixes taken to move fast. One line each: file, what was hacked, what the real fix is.
`/potato-start product` starts from this list.

- sim/episode.py: re-grip at the chosen force after the test lift. A real Franka Hand needs a second `grasp()` call; model that delay and the brief release. Real fix: decide the force from camera features before grasping, or measure the regrasp on hardware.
- sim/episode.py: the wrist F/T site plus Gaussian noise stands in for Franka's joint-torque-based `O_F_ext_hat_K` estimate. Real fix: estimate the external wrench from simulated joint torques the way libfranka does.
- sim/franka.py: torsional friction uses an assumed contact-patch radius of 6 mm (`ContactParams.torsion_radius`), which drives tilt failures. Real fix: calibrate against Franka Hand pad measurements.
- sim/franka.py: the conveyor is static (items spawn at the pick point). Real fix: a moving belt only if belt motion matters for the grasp.
- scripts/spike_cudagraph.py: re-compiles `TabPFNV3p5.forward` from its source with the `.item()` class-count sync replaced by a fixed `_fixed_num_classes = 2`, and replaces `model.forward` with a CUDA-graph wrapper found via `gc`. Both depend on TabPFN 10.2.0 internals. Real fix: an upstream option to pass the class count, and a supported hook for graph capture (or the JAX port).
- jax_tabpfn/refit_demo.py: runs torch and JAX in one interpreter by appending the torch env's site-packages to `PYTHONPATH` of `.venv-jax`. Real fix: one env with torch cu132 + `jax[cuda13]` (they coexist on the RTX 4050 as tested), once the main session agrees.
- jax_tabpfn/export.py: supports only 1 estimator, all-numeric columns and TabPFN-3.5's default preprocessing (transform `none`, no categoricals, T=1, no balancing); anything else raises. Real fix: port the remaining TabPFN preprocessing steps (quantile, SVD, categorical encoding) and ensembles.
- jax_tabpfn/runtime.py: the context size is a static shape, so a new context row count compiles once (15–110 s, then persistent cache). Real fix: mask padded train rows in the ICL/decoder attention and pad the ECDF buckets, so contexts can be bucketed like queries.
- jax_tabpfn/model.py: the fast-mode decoder asserts at most 64 classes (one flash-attention call of one-hot values). Real fix: loop over 64-class chunks like `_class_attention`.
- scripts/verify_jax_port.py: carries a copy of the CUDA-graph wrapper and `.item()` patch from `scripts/spike_cudagraph.py`, because that script runs its experiment at import. Real fix: move the wrapper into an importable module (or drop it if the JAX path wins), and have both scripts import it.
- sim/products.py: tooling deviations from the researched lines. Blister has 1 cup instead of 2 × Ø16; bar has a Ø25 round flat cup instead of a 20×45 rectangular bellows; cheese wedge is an equal-area box with the measured CoM offset on a flat SPF 40 (research: Ø40–50 bellows); pouch film drawn into the cup is not modelled. Real fix: a rectangular lip ring in sim/cup.py, a mesh wedge, and a membrane product.
- sim/products.py: lip ring stiffness, cup mass and travel of flat and bellows cups are scaled from the calibrated SPB1 20 [E]. Volumes are vendor data (SPF, SPB1, SPG; the Ø28 bellows interpolated, SPB2f 50 extrapolated); effective areas are vendor data except bar and digestive [E]. Real fix: vendor or bench stiffness data per cup, and the effective areas of the real bar and digestive cups.
- sim/products.py + sim/cup.py: the SPG bell wall is 16 capsules on axial slide joints whose stiffness is the thin wall's bending (Gent E of 45 Shore A silicone, 1 mm wall [E], strip cantilever from the neck to the seal circle); the cone's hoop stiffness and the wall's own flaring are not modelled, and a seated praline rests on the joint stops. Cone angle and neck read off the datasheet drawing [D]. Real fix: a shell model of the bell (or measured capture tolerance and tilt stiffness) to replace the lumped wall.
- sim/products.py: generator = ceil(Ds/21.4) Si08-3 cartridges in parallel [E]; regulator modelled as a 2 kPa proportional band below p_set [E]. Real fix: the integrator's actual generator and regulator curves.
- sim/products.py: seal classes (smooth 70–75, film 55–70, porous 20–50 kPa) are inverted to roughness gap / porosity; wear is a parallel viscous leak through the lip growing linearly with cycles, calibrated per product to 15 % leak-down at 750k cycles (a CMMS rule, not a vendor figure), plus −15 % friction; film wrinkle leak 2 µm per mm of pick offset; pouch slosh 70 % of mass at 2–5 Hz, ζ 0.1. All [E]. Real fix: a real vacuum-sensor log per product (model-suction.md §7).
- policy/policies.py: in the replays the learned policies refit every 5 picks (a live line would refresh its speed map about once a second, every 2–3 picks). Real fix: refit per pick and swap the context into the JAX runtime (~20 ms).
- jax_tabpfn/ + scripts/spike_cudagraph.py: still run on the toy log `sim/delta_toy.py`; the 3.7 ms JAX latency was measured there (same column count). Real fix: re-run the JAX parity and benchmark on `results/logs/`, then delete `sim/delta_toy.py`.
- docs/explainer/delta-pick-line.html: the in-browser animation runs a toy physics model with a Bayesian stand-in for TabPFN (labelled on the page). Real fix: drive the page from the measured replays in `results/eval/`.
