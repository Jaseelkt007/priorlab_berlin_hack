The accuracy is there. Speed decides where it fits.

Our JAX port of TabPFN: 3.7 ms instead of 30.9 ms (PyTorch, laptop GPU), same answers: 0 of 3,825 decisions differ.

Where it breaks: a sudden 80° turn. TabPFN is 5 mm off 83 ms later and the fly falls. Sudden turns are lost within 30–80 ms at every context size, up to 1M rows.

  • Crane: the −35 % is against anti-sway set for the wrong sling. With the right sling typed in, industry anti-sway is as good.
  • Pick line: it drops the first unseen heavy bar, then slows every one after. A fixed 40 % chosen in hindsight does slightly better (155.6/min).
  • Drone: the alarm is a tie: the IMM catches 100 % of breaches, IMM + TabPFN 97 %.
  • Everything except the lab crane is simulation.

04 · Takeaway

A robot's own log is enough.

01

It adapts by adding rows

The crane's error drops 3× after 5 s of a new load. A 20 % heavier fly: −44 % error after 16 example flights. The pick line slows down after a single drop.

02

It knows when it is unsure

Calibration error 1.1 % over 2,700 picks, against 4.0 % for boosted trees. Every forecast comes with a range, so decisions can price the risk.

03

Speed is the frontier

Our JAX port decides a pick in 2.35 ms, inside the 35 ms seal window. The same speed-up is what the drone and the fly need next.
