01 · The idea

Write the robot's log as a table. TabPFN reads it as examples.

Sensors + outcomeswhat the machine already measures
One row per momentthe past rows are the context
TabPFNpretrained once; adapting means adding rows
Prediction + rangea full distribution, not a point
Decisionspeed, anti-sway, alarm, controls

Our JAX port of TabPFN: 3.7 ms instead of 30.9 ms in PyTorch on a laptop GPU, with the same answers (0 of 3,825 decisions differ).

Where it breaks

A sudden 80° turn: TabPFN is 5 mm off 83 ms later, and the fly falls.

  • Flybot: sudden turns are lost within 30–80 ms at every context size, up to 1M rows.
  • Crane: −35 % is against the wrong sling; with the right one, industry anti-sway is as good.
  • Delta picker: drops the first unseen heavy bar; a fixed 40 % in hindsight does slightly better.
  • Drone: the alarm is a tie, 100 % vs 97 % of breaches caught. All but the lab crane is simulation.

04 · Takeaway

A robot's own log is enough.

01Adapts by adding rows

3× lower crane error after 5 s of a new load. −44 % fly error after 16 flights of a heavier fly. The delta picker slows after one drop.

02Knows when it is unsure

Calibration error 1.1 % over 2,700 picks, against 4.0 % for boosted trees. Every forecast comes with a range.

03Speed is the frontier

2.35 ms per pick decision with our JAX port. The drone and the Flybot need the same speed-up next.
